# How to Filter YouTube Videos by Views Per Day

**Quick answer:** In YouTube Filter Pro, set **Views per day** min/max on the video filters row, run **Search**, then review matches in the results panel.

Long story short, I got tired of opening twenty tabs just to check upload dates and view counts on YouTube search.

---

## What YouTube lets you do today

YouTube displays total views and rough age, but not views per day as a filter. You would have to calculate velocity yourself video by video.

---

## How to do it with YouTube Filter Pro

1. Run a normal YouTube search for your topic.
2. Click **Filter** next to the search bar (YouTube Filter Pro).
3. Set the filters described below in the modal (Video or Channel tabs).
4. Click **Search** in the footer and open **View Results** when the panel appears.
5. Leave the tab open while the extension scans more results (complex filters can take up to a minute).

Open **Views per day** and set a **minimum** (e.g. 500) or a band (500–10,000) for your niche.

---

## Example workflow

For `Excel tips`, a floor of 200 views/day removes dead uploads while keeping mid-tier tutorials visible.

---

## Tips and common mistakes

### Pair with publish date

Views/day without a date window mixes 10-year-old hits with new uploads.

### Check the data bar

Enable the video stats row in Settings to see views/day on each match.

## FAQ

### How is views per day calculated?

From total views and publish date for each video the extension enriches.

### Can I sort by views per day too?

Yes—see the dedicated sort guide in this series.

### Does it work on Shorts?

Use the Shorts filter row to include or exclude them first.
