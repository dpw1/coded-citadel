# How to Find YouTube Videos About to Go Viral (Early Signal Filter)

**Quick answer:** Filter YouTube search to **last 7 days**, **views/day ≥ 3,000**, and **subscribers ≤ 5,000**, then sort filtered results by **views per day** descending. That surfaces smaller channels with unusual velocity; it is research, not a prediction.

I built [YouTube Filter Pro](/blog/youtube-filter-chrome-extension-coding) because YouTube search never gave me the combo of filters I wanted in one place.

---

## What YouTube lets you do today

YouTube shows upload date and view counts on each video, but there is no built-in “viral radar” and no way to combine recency, daily velocity, and channel size in one search. Sorting by view count alone favors old giants.

---

## How to do it with YouTube Filter Pro

1. Run a normal YouTube search for your topic.
2. Click **Filter** next to the search bar (YouTube Filter Pro).
3. Set the filters described below in the modal (Video or Channel tabs).
4. Click **Search** in the footer and open **View Results** when the panel appears.
5. Leave the tab open while the extension scans more results (complex filters can take up to a minute).

**Filters to set:**
- **Publish date:** Last 7 days
- **Views per day:** minimum **3,000**
- **Subscribers:** maximum **5,000**

**After results load:** open the sort menu in the results panel and choose **Views per day** (highest first).

## Example workflow

Search `home gym setup`. With the filters above you might see a 4-day-old video from a 2.1k-sub channel doing ~4.5k views/day—worth watching for format and hook, not proof it will hit 1M views.

## Tips and common mistakes

### Tighten with title excludes

Add title **exclude** terms like `live`, `shorts`, or `reaction` if they pollute your niche.

### This is not a guarantee

High views/day on a new upload can mean promotion, controversy, or a one-off. Treat it as a lead, not destiny.

### Empty results

If nothing matches, widen subscriber max or lower views/day slightly. Ultra-tight filters on broad queries often return zero rows.

## FAQ

### Does this predict viral videos?

No. It prioritizes recent uploads with strong daily view velocity on relatively small channels so you can research trends earlier.

### Why cap subscribers at 5,000?

Large channels skew averages. The cap keeps attention on creators who are still breaking out.

### Do I need YouTube Filter Pro?

Yes for this combined filter on search results. The extension runs client-side on youtube.com.

### Will filtering use my free search quota?

A search that returns **zero** matches does not consume a search credit; matches do.
