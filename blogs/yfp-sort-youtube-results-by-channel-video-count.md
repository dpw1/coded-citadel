# How to Sort YouTube Results by How Many Videos a Channel Has

**Quick answer:** **Sort → Channel videos** (total videos on the channel) after filtering.

I built [YouTube Filter Pro](/blog/youtube-filter-chrome-extension-coding) because YouTube search never gave me the combo of filters I wanted in one place.

---

## What YouTube lets you do today

YouTube does not offer this sort on search.

---

## How to do it with YouTube Filter Pro

1. Run a normal YouTube search for your topic.
2. Click **Filter** next to the search bar (YouTube Filter Pro).
3. Set the filters described below in the modal (Video or Channel tabs).
4. Click **Search** in the footer and open **View Results** when the panel appears.
5. Leave the tab open while the extension scans more results (complex filters can take up to a minute).

Use **Channel videos** in the sort list.

---

## Example workflow

Sort ascending on `sourdough` search to find first-time bakers with few uploads.
