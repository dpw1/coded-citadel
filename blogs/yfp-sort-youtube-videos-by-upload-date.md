# How to Sort YouTube Search Results by Upload Date (Newest First)

**Quick answer:** Run your filters, open **View Results**, use the sort control, and pick **Date** (toggle direction for newest vs oldest).

When I shipped YouTube Filter Pro, this exact workflow is what I had in mind.

---

## What YouTube lets you do today

YouTube search sort is global for the page, not for a custom filtered subset. Relevance sort also buries fresh uploads.

---

## How to do it with YouTube Filter Pro

1. Run a normal YouTube search for your topic.
2. Click **Filter** next to the search bar (YouTube Filter Pro).
3. Set the filters described below in the modal (Video or Channel tabs).
4. Click **Search** in the footer and open **View Results** when the panel appears.
5. Leave the tab open while the extension scans more results (complex filters can take up to a minute).

In the **filtered results** header, open **Sort** and choose **Date**.

---

## Example workflow

Filter `AI news` to last 30 days, then sort by date to read this week’s uploads in order.

---

## Tips and common mistakes

### Filter first

Sorting thousands of irrelevant videos is pointless—narrow with publish date or title includes first.

### Final re-sort

When enrichment finishes, the panel may re-sort once so metrics are complete.

---

## FAQ

### Does this change YouTube’s own sort?

No—it only orders rows inside the Filter Pro results panel.

### Can I sort oldest first?

Yes—flip sort direction in the sort menu.
