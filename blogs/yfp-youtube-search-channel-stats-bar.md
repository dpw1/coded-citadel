# How to See Subscriber Count and Country on YouTube Search Results

**Quick answer:** Enable **channel data bar** in Settings on search/results rows.

I am not saying YouTube search is useless, but for narrow research it wastes a lot of time without extra filters.

---

## What YouTube lets you do today

YouTube’s built-in search filters cover basics like upload date and content type. They do not expose view bands, engagement rates, channel metadata rules, or a dedicated filtered results panel with export.

---

## How to do it with YouTube Filter Pro

1. Run a normal YouTube search for your topic.
2. Click **Filter** next to the search bar (YouTube Filter Pro).
3. Set the filters described below in the modal (Video or Channel tabs).
4. Click **Search** in the footer and open **View Results** when the panel appears.
5. Leave the tab open while the extension scans more results (complex filters can take up to a minute).

**This post:** Enable **channel data bar** in Settings on search/results rows.

## Tips and common mistakes

### Start broad, then tighten

If you get zero matches, loosen one constraint (usually views or subscribers) before giving up.

### Leave the tab open

The extension keeps scanning as YouTube loads more search results.

## FAQ

### Do I need a YouTube account?

You need to be on youtube.com in Chrome with the extension installed; login matches your normal YouTube session.

### Does data leave my browser?

Core filtering runs client-side on the page. Optional external enrichment may fetch public metadata—see the extension privacy note.

### Where is the Filter button?

Next to the YouTube search bar on supported pages (search results, home, watch, channel pages).
